// about

An interviewer who's always ready for you

InterviewAce AI is a conversational coach built specifically for software engineering candidates. Instead of static PDFs and one-size-fits-all question banks, you get a chatbot that responds to your background, adapts to the round you're preparing for, and keeps the session moving like a real interview.

Open the chat, tell it what you're preparing for — a DSA round, an HR screen, a resume review, or a specific company's loop — and InterviewAce AI takes it from there: asking questions, reviewing your answers, and pointing you toward what to study next.
